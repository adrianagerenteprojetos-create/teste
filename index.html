Scientific Evidence

What the Research Actually Supports

It's important to separate two different things: research on individual ingredients, and research on Audifort as a finished product. Ingredients like grape seed extract and green tea extract have a body of general nutrition research behind them, largely focused on antioxidant activity and cardiovascular or circulatory markers. GABA has been studied in relation to relaxation and stress response. None of this research was designed to test hearing outcomes, and none of it evaluated this specific formula, dosage, or combination.

As of this review, we found no published, peer-reviewed clinical trial of Audifort itself. That's common for supplements in this category — formulating around individually-studied ingredients is standard industry practice — but it also means any claim about how the product performs for a given individual is based on the ingredients' general research profile, not on direct evidence for the finished product. Anyone considering it for a specific hearing concern should treat that as a meaningful gap, not a technicality.
